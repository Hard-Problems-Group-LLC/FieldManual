# Guidelines

Guidelines are short, practical heuristics for recurring design and
engineering decisions. They complement the normative material under
`standards-and-practices/core/`; they do not replace it.

Treat a guideline as a default to evaluate in context, not as proof that a
design is correct. A guideline should:

- identify its scope;
- state the recommended default;
- explain important exceptions;
- distinguish evidence from analogy;
- cite authoritative or durable sources when available; and
- include a review date when its evidence or platform assumptions may change.

Executable checks for guideline adoption belong to consuming projects or
future verifier submodules.
